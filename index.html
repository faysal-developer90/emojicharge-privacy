🔋 EmojiCharge Privacy Policy

Developer: Fixpertify
Effective date: November 18, 2026

EmojiCharge ("the app") is developed by Fixpertify. This policy explains what information is handled when you use the app.

Summary

EmojiCharge does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal or device data. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Everything happens on your device.

Accessibility Service

The app uses Android's AccessibilityService API only to draw your emoji battery and custom status bar on top of the system status bar. The service:

  • reads only the type, size and focus state of system windows, to hide itself while the notification panel is open,
  • does not read text or any other content shown on your screen,
  • does not monitor what you type or which apps you use,
  • does not send any information off your device.

You can turn the service off at any time in Settings → Accessibility.

Information used on your device only

To show its status bar, the app reads the following on your device. None of it is stored or leaves your device:

  • Battery level and charging state, for the battery indicator.
  • Wi-Fi and mobile signal strength, for the signal icons on the custom status bar.
  • The time, for the clock on the custom status bar.

Hiding system icons (optional)

If you grant the optional "WRITE_SECURE_SETTINGS" permission yourself from a computer, the app can hide the phone's own battery and other status bar icons so they don't overlap yours. It changes only the system's list of hidden status bar icons, and restores the icons it hid when you turn the feature or the app off.

Settings

Your preferences (chosen emoji, colors, sizes) are stored only on your device.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.
